Ticket 1 · parser · 379 reports

The shared date parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes dates day first (locales.ts keeps the directory), so invites the tenants sent come back flipped: 7 April runs on 4 July. Eleven tenants, all three channels (app 204 · email 150 · form 25), April → October. This is the bug the desk already knows, and the reason #4471 exists. Filing note: desk-notes.json lets five reports the desk read as a one-day move stay where they are filed, as footnotes under this cause.

Ticket 2 · hint · 33 reports

All of these came through the booking-v3 form. Its hint prints MM/DD/YYYY, so guests type month first, and the form reads what arrived the tenant's way — day first. Same flipped day, wrong entry and wrong hint, so a different fix. The three tenants in this group — alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour — are exactly the three running booking-v3. Every other tenant runs booking-v2, whose hint says DD/MM/YYYY, so their form reports belong to the parser cause.

While the desk worked, engineering moved: #4471 closed on 05 October.

The two tickets the desk filed closed the engineering way: parser completed (the fix landed), hint as not planned (the form retired).
